\section{Ablation Study}
\label{sec:ablation}

Two families of ablation apply here, and keeping them apart matters. A
\emph{defence ablation} removes or substitutes a control in the target
and asks what the attack surface becomes; three of the five suites were
designed as ablations in this sense, with the poison-budget sweep
applying the idea to the attacker's resource instead. A
\emph{measurement ablation} removes a component of the assessment
apparatus and asks what the study would have reported without it --- the
family usually left implicit, and where the methodological claims of
Secs.~\ref{sec:architecture} and~\ref{sec:methodology} are either cashed
out or exposed as decoration. Every entry below is either a measurement
taken in these runs or an arithmetic counterfactual computed exactly
from retained per-attempt records; nothing is an estimate. The one
ablation that would be most informative is unavailable: model scale is
held constant throughout, which matters especially for poisoning, where
susceptibility rises significantly with parameter count across 24 models
in eight series~\cite[Table~2]{bowen2025scaling}.

Four things are visible here that no single subsection of
Sec.~\ref{sec:results} shows. \textbf{The prompt-level block is
monotone; the layer block is not:} block~(a) falls at every step, while
block~(b) contains two rows with a \emph{positive} $\Delta$ and one at
exactly zero, which --- since only the layer varies --- says the
configuration carrying the defence is no safer than carrying none.
\textbf{The layer, not the effort, predicts the outcome:} grouping
block~(b) by layer, the output-filter stages average $0.000$ on the
literal-leak measure, the context stage $0.000$, the input-filter stages
$0.121/0.333/0.061/0.030$, and the prompt stages $0.242/0.182$. The
ordering \emph{output $<$ input $<$ prompt} is exactly the ordering of
how late in the pipeline the control acts and how little it depends on
the model's cooperation --- and the output-filter zeros are qualified,
since 12 of the study's 97 leaks escaped in renderings a
single-representation filter does not match. \textbf{Availability is the
input filter's hidden $\Delta$:} B7's $-0.061$ is bought by rejecting
$54.5\,\%$ of all requests, while B8 reaches a better $-0.091$ rejecting
nothing, so the block's apparent security gain is not a gain at a fixed
cost. \textbf{And the budget curve's threshold is not the onset:}
block~(d) shows three $\pm 0.000$ rows before a step that never moves
again, while Table~\ref{tab:dose} shows the trigger's
$P(\text{positive})$ rising $0.230 \to 0.497$ across those same rows. An
ablation over the attacker's resource is only interpretable alongside an
instrument that reads continuous output.

\textbf{The ground-truth channel is not an improvement, it is the
precondition.} Its removal does not degrade the two largest suites by
some margin; it deletes them, because the alternative to abstaining is
asserting ``no leak'' on 546 attempts about which the detector knows
nothing. What looks like conservative book-keeping in
Sec.~\ref{sec:arch:detectors} determines whether the assessment produces
a result or an artefact.

\textbf{Two distortions are large enough to invert conclusions.}
Substituting the stock detector multiplies reported violations by
$3.4$, so an operator acting on that number would triage 147
non-violations; consuming the application's own flag instead of
cross-tabulating it would have reported $0.762$ where the independent
detector measured $0.000$ over the same 21 attempts. These are opposite
errors --- one from a detector indifferent to policy, one from a signal
encoding the application's belief about itself --- and the only thing
separating them from the reported numbers is that both comparison
signals were carried alongside rather than consumed.

\textbf{The harness detail is a first-class control.} The
plugin-instance cache reset is four lines and reads like an
implementation footnote, yet without it the persona sweep is five
repetitions of L1 and the ladder ten of B0, and
Tables~\ref{tab:technique} and \ref{tab:ablationdefence}(a,\,b) would
each be a single configuration reported as a trend. A sweep that
silently does not sweep produces confident numbers that are internally
consistent and entirely wrong.

\textbf{Three components were guarantees, not corrections}, changing
nothing in these runs. Their value is asymmetric: each is cheap, and
each converts a silent misattribution into a visible exclusion when the
condition it guards does occur. A study that reports only the components
that moved a number describes the apparatus it happened to need rather
than the one that made the result trustworthy --- which is why the
\emph{latent} rows are in the table. \textbf{And sensitivity and
precision trade in both directions:} removing all obfuscation-aware
matching loses 12 genuine leaks and the evidence base for M-02, while
removing only the unbounded whitespace-stripped branch eliminates the
one confirmed false positive at the cost of two genuine plural
renderings. Recording \emph{which} rendering matched on every hit is
what made both rows computable without re-running anything.
