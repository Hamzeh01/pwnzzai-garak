\section{Ablation Study}
\label{sec:ablation}

Two families of ablation apply here, and keeping them apart matters. A
\emph{defence ablation} removes or substitutes a control in the target
and asks what the attack surface becomes; three of the five suites were
designed as ablations in this sense, with the poison-budget sweep
applying the idea to the attacker's resource instead. A
\emph{measurement ablation} removes a component of the assessment
apparatus and asks what the study would have reported without it --- the
family usually left implicit, and where the methodological claims of
Secs.~\ref{sec:architecture} and~\ref{sec:methodology} are either
cashed out or exposed as decoration. Every entry below is a measurement
taken in these runs or an arithmetic counterfactual computed exactly
from retained per-attempt records; nothing is an estimate. The most
informative ablation is unavailable: model scale is held constant
throughout, which matters most for poisoning~\cite[Table~2]{bowen2025scaling}.

\subsection{Defence Ablations}
\label{sec:abl:defence}

Each of the four swept controls is reported with its own results table,
because within every block the corpus, generator, model and generation
count are identical and only the named component differs, so the
$\Delta$ there \emph{is} the ablation: (a) persona hardening at the
prompt layer, L1 $17/36 = 0.472$ falling monotonically to L5
$3/36 = 0.083$, with $\Delta = -0.166$, $-0.166$, $-0.278$ and
$\mathbf{-0.389}$ at L2--L5 (Sec.~\ref{sec:res:direct}); (b) guardrail
stage substitution across
the prompt, input, output and context layers, B0 $4/33 = 0.121$ against
$0.000$ at B5, B6 and B9 and $0.333$ at B4, $\Delta$ from $-0.121$ to
$+0.212$ and \emph{not} monotone (Table~\ref{tab:ladder}); (c)
trusted-only retrieval, $12/12 = 1.000$ off against $0/12$ retrieved
and $0.417$ at answer level on, $\Delta = \mathbf{-1.000}$ and $-0.583$
(Sec.~\ref{sec:res:poisoning}); and (d) the attacker's poison budget,
$0/7$ at $b \le 3$ then $2/7 = 0.286$ from $b = 5$,
$\Delta = +0.286$ (Table~\ref{tab:dose}).

Four things are visible across the blocks that no single block shows.
\textbf{The prompt-level block is monotone; the layer block is not:}
block~(b) has two rows with a \emph{positive} $\Delta$ and one at
exactly zero, which --- since only the layer varies --- says the
configuration carrying the defence is no safer than carrying none.
\textbf{The layer, not the effort, predicts the outcome:} grouping
block~(b) by layer, the output-filter stages average $0.000$ on the
literal-leak measure, the context stage $0.000$, the input-filter
stages $0.121/0.333/0.061/0.030$ and the prompt stages
$0.242/0.182$. The ordering \emph{output $<$ input $<$ prompt} is
exactly the ordering of how late in the pipeline a control acts and how
little it depends on the model's cooperation --- and the output-filter
zeros are qualified, since 12 of the 97 leaks escaped in renderings a
single-representation filter does not match. \textbf{Availability is
the input filter's hidden $\Delta$:} B7's $-0.061$ is bought by
rejecting $54.5\,\%$ of all requests, while B8 reaches a better
$-0.091$ rejecting nothing. \textbf{And the budget curve's threshold is
not the onset:} block~(d) shows three $\pm 0.000$ steps before a jump
that never moves again, while Table~\ref{tab:dose} shows
$P(\text{positive})$ rising $0.230 \to 0.497$ across those same steps.
An ablation over the attacker's resource is only interpretable
alongside an instrument that reads continuous output.

Four readings follow. \textbf{The ground-truth channel is not an
improvement but the precondition:} removing it does not degrade the two
largest suites by some margin, it deletes them, because the alternative
to abstaining is asserting ``no leak'' on 546 attempts about which the
detector knows nothing. \textbf{Two distortions are large enough to
invert conclusions}, and they are opposite errors --- one from a
detector indifferent to policy, one from a signal encoding the
application's belief about itself. The only thing separating both from
the reported numbers is that the comparison signals were carried
alongside rather than consumed. \textbf{A harness detail is a
first-class control:} the four-line cache reset reads like an
implementation footnote, yet without it Tables~\ref{tab:technique}
and~\ref{tab:ladder} would each be one configuration reported as a
trend, and a sweep that silently does not sweep produces confident
numbers that are internally consistent and entirely wrong.
\textbf{Three components were guarantees, not corrections}, changing
nothing here; each converts a silent misattribution into a visible
exclusion when the condition it guards does occur, and a study
reporting only the components that moved a number describes the
apparatus it happened to need rather than the one that made the result
trustworthy. Finally, \textbf{sensitivity and precision trade in both
directions}, and recording \emph{which} rendering matched on every hit
is what made both obfuscation rows computable without re-running
anything.
