# Phase 7 Mitigation Matrix

Mitigations are tied to the evidenced attack-chain step. Residual risk is expected after the control and does not imply guaranteed prevention.

| ID | Finding | Chain step | Class / layer | Effort | Residual | Recommendation | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `M-PI-01` | `F-001` | Model-visible input changes prohibited behavior | preventive / application | medium | medium | Keep authorization and security decisions outside the model; enforce allowed actions and structured output policy in deterministic code. [OWASP guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) | Repeat the frozen role, encoded, multi-turn, and conflict cases; require 0 confirmed marker emissions and preserve 0/9 benign false positives. |
| `M-PI-02` | `F-001` | Untrusted direct or transformed text is interpreted as authority | preventive / application/model | medium | medium | Explicitly separate and label untrusted content, canonicalize encoded input before policy evaluation, and apply least-privilege capabilities. [OWASP guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) | Run semantically equivalent encoded and multi-turn regression cases under the same frozen detector and verify safe behavior. |
| `M-PI-03` | `F-001` | A prohibited model output reaches the caller | detective / application | low | medium | Validate model output, monitor canary/DLP signals, and log repeated policy-triggering inputs without treating keyword blocking as sufficient. [OWASP guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) | Inject synthetic detector fixtures offline and verify alerts, redaction, and no raw secret or session data in logs. |
| `M-POI-01` | `F-002` | Ordinary caller submits labels and triggers retraining | preventive / application/data | medium | medium | Require authenticated, least-privilege authorization and human approval for data ingestion, labeling, retraining, and model promotion. [OWASP guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm042025-data-and-model-poisoning/) | Repeat all nonzero workflows as an ordinary caller and require rejection before training while an approved administrative path remains auditable. |
| `M-POI-02` | `F-002` | Untrusted samples enter the training set | preventive / data | high | medium | Record immutable provenance; enforce label-consistency, duplicate, outlier, trigger, and per-source influence checks before acceptance. [OWASP guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm042025-data-and-model-poisoning/) | Submit the frozen mislabeled samples and verify quarantine plus a complete provenance/audit event without changing the clean dataset. |
| `M-POI-03` | `F-002` | A degraded model is eligible for promotion | preventive/detective / model/data | medium | low | Gate promotion on the fixed clean holdout, targeted flip checks, feature-weight drift, and explicit accuracy-degradation thresholds. [OWASP guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm042025-data-and-model-poisoning/) | Require promotion failure for any model with at least 1/4 flips or at least 1/4 clean-accuracy degradation. |
| `M-POI-04` | `F-002` | Poisoned data or model state must be recovered | recovery / data/model | medium | low | Version datasets and models, retain signed clean checkpoints, monitor drift, and provide a tested rollback to the last approved pair. [OWASP guidance](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm042025-data-and-model-poisoning/) | Promote a synthetic failing candidate in an isolated test, block it, restore the clean version, and reproduce 4/4 holdout accuracy. |

Prompt changes and keyword blacklists may be supporting controls, but they do not independently close either demonstrated chain.
